Robust network presence detection for Homey Pro without requiring any companion app or background battery drain on your phone.

Network Presence monitors smartphones and connected devices on your local Wi-Fi network using unicast ARP and TCP probing. It accurately detects when you arrive home or leave, providing responsive presence states for your smart home automations.

FEATURES

Fast arrival detection when devices connect to Wi-Fi.
Configurable departure grace period to prevent false away triggers while modern phones enter deep sleep.
Direct link to registered Homey users.
Device-level Flow cards for triggers, conditions, and on-demand polling actions.
Completely local operation with zero cloud dependencies.
